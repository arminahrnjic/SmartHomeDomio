Apartment owners are rarely there in person when a guest arrives or leaves. Every small thing you can handle remotely means fewer trips, fewer messages, and less stress.

## What guests actually notice

Guests don't notice smart technology — they notice when something doesn't work as it should. Curtains that open on their own in the morning or close in the evening feel like a thoughtful touch, not a gadget.

## Fewer reasons to show up in person

You don't need to come by just to close the curtains before a new group of guests, or to check that everything's ready. It's all handled from the app, wherever you are.

## One control for multiple units

If you manage several apartments, the same account and app cover all of them — no separate remote for every unit.

## Installation with no permanent changes

It mounts directly onto the existing rail, with no drilling into the wall — suitable even for rented spaces where permanent changes aren't allowed.

For apartments and weekend homes where being there in person isn't always possible, the [Smart Curtain Robot](/en/proizvodi/smart-curtain-robot) with WiFi control solves exactly this problem.
