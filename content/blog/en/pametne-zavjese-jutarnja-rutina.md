Waking up to an alarm is jarring. Waking up to light is natural. A small change in your morning routine can be the difference between a tired start to the day and a calm one.

## 1. Natural light wakes the body, not the alarm

The body responds to light, not sound. When the curtains slowly open before the alarm goes off, the brain begins waking up gradually — without the sudden jolt out of sleep.

## 2. One less thing to remember at night

No more "did someone close the curtains." Set the schedule once, and it runs on its own every day.

## 3. The same feeling even when you're away from home

Curtains can open and close on schedule even while you're traveling — the house looks "lived in," without anyone physically stopping by.

## 4. Quiet that goes unnoticed

The motor runs quietly, quiet enough not to wake anyone too early, fast enough that you're not left waiting.

## 5. Control stays in your hands

The app and voice control mean changing the schedule takes a few seconds — no tools, no calling a technician.
